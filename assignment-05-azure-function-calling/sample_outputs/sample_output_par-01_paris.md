## Paris, France - 3-Day Itinerary
**Style:** Cultural | **Budget Tier:** Moderate | **Estimated Cost:** $1,250.00 USD

### Daily Schedule

#### Day 1: Historical Heart & River Romance
- **Morning:** Explore Île de la Cité, Sainte-Chapelle stained glass, and Notre-Dame exterior.
- **Afternoon:** Guided visit through the Louvre Museum focusing on the Denon and Sully wings.
- **Evening:** Sunset Seine River cruise followed by dinner in Saint-Germain-des-Prés.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Duck confit with pommes sarladaises and tarte tatin.

#### Day 2: Bohemian Heights & Impressionist Art
- **Morning:** Climb Montmartre hill to Sacré-Cœur Basilica and discover Place du Tertre.
- **Afternoon:** Tour Musée d'Orsay to admire Monet, Degas, and Van Gogh masterpieces.
- **Evening:** Walk through Tuileries Garden and enjoy dusk views from Pont Alexandre III.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** French onion soup (soupe à l'oignon gratinée) and crêpes.

#### Day 3: Royal Grandeur & Chic Marais
- **Morning:** Visit the Eiffel Tower summit and walk through Champ de Mars.
- **Afternoon:** Browse contemporary galleries and historic mansions in Le Marais.
- **Evening:** Farewell tasting dinner at a classic Parisian bistro near Place des Vosges.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Coq au vin with Burgundy Pinot Noir and macarons.

### Local Tips & Logistics
- Use the Paris Metro Navigo Easy pass for seamless underground transit.
- Book Louvre and Eiffel Tower time slots at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Always greet shopkeepers with 'Bonjour Madame/Monsieur' upon entering.
