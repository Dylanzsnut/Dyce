## New York City, USA - 4-Day Itinerary
**Style:** Leisure | **Budget Tier:** Budget | **Estimated Cost:** $850.00 USD

### Daily Schedule

#### Day 1: Midtown Icons & Urban Parks
- **Morning:** Walk Central Park from Bethesda Terrace to Strawberry Fields with a picnic bagel.
- **Afternoon:** Marvel at New York Public Library Rose Main Reading Room and Bryant Park.
- **Evening:** Take in the neon lights of Times Square and Grand Central Terminal constellation ceiling.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Everything bagel with scallion cream cheese and classic New York slice pizza.

#### Day 2: Downtown History & Harbor Views
- **Morning:** Free Staten Island Ferry round-trip ride offering prime views of Statue of Liberty.
- **Afternoon:** Reflect at the 9/11 Memorial Plaza and walk through Wall Street and Oculus.
- **Evening:** Stroll across the iconic Brooklyn Bridge during sunset into DUMBO.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Coal-fired brick oven margherita pizza in Brooklyn.

#### Day 3: Elevated Architecture & Vibrant Neighborhoods
- **Morning:** Walk the High Line elevated park from Hudson Yards to Chelsea Market.
- **Afternoon:** Explore Greenwich Village brownstones and Washington Square Park street performers.
- **Evening:** Browse bookshops in Soho and dine at historic Chinatown spots.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Steamed pork soup dumplings (xiaolongbao) and warm Portuguese egg tarts.

#### Day 4: Arts, Culture & River Vistas
- **Morning:** Pay-what-you-wish or free morning entry to neighborhood cultural galleries.
- **Afternoon:** Walk along Brooklyn Heights Promenade with panoramic Lower Manhattan skyline views.
- **Evening:** Dessert and skyline night gazing from Gantry Plaza State Park in Queens.
- **Cuisine Highlight:** Classic pastrami on rye sandwich and black-and-white cookies.

### Local Tips & Logistics
- Use OMNY contactless tap-to-pay on subways; after 12 rides in a Monday-Sunday week, all additional rides are free.
- Enjoy free iconic views from the Staten Island Ferry instead of expensive boat tours.
- Walk across the bridges instead of taking taxis to avoid heavy traffic delays.
